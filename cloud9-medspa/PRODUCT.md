# PRODUCT

**Design read:** For women (and some men) across Keller, Alliance and North Fort Worth who want to look rested and feel looked-after, not sold to. They must feel calm, safe and personally known. The one action: **book a free consult** (call, or leave a number for a call-back).

## Audience
- 28 to 60, mostly women, living or working near Alliance / Keller / Heritage / Park Glen.
- Arrives on a phone from Instagram, Google Maps or a friend's text. Short sessions, one thumb.
- Two moods: "I know what I want" (Botox price, membership) and "I'm not sure what I need" (the plan tool).
- Sizeable Spanish-speaking population in Tarrant County, so English + Spanish.

## Voice
Warm, plain, a little dreamy, never pushy. Short sentences. Speaks like Sara at the front desk:
specific about price and time, honest about what something will and won't do. No hype words
("revolutionary", "flawless", "anti-aging miracle"). No before/after promises.

## Primary action
"Book a free consult": in nav, hero, after the depth section, after the menu, in clubs, in the
plan result, in visit and footer, plus the sticky mobile bar (Call / Free consult).
Secondary: tap-to-call (972) 897-2355.

## Constraints
- Regulated: medical aesthetics in Texas. Modest claims, "results vary", consult first,
  prescription treatments only after medical evaluation. No weight-loss outcome testimonials.
- Facts were gathered through search summaries only (network blocked). Everything unverified
  is flagged in content files and shown only in review builds.
- No real photography or video was reachable. Visuals are honest illustrations and diagrams,
  labelled as such, with media slots ready for the owner's real photos.
